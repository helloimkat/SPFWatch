SPF Watch

Sunny watches the UV index and taps your wrist when your sunscreen has worn off. The reapply timer shortens as UV climbs: every 2 hours at low and moderate UV, 100 minutes at high, 90 at very high and 80 at extreme. Swimming cuts it short.

Try it

  • Scroll over the watch or swipe up and down to turn the Digital Crown between Now, Forecast and Today.
  • Click the crown to see the watch face with the Smart Stack widget and complications.
  • Let time run and a reminder arrives as a Short Look, then a Long Look with actions.
